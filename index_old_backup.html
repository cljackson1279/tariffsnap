How to Calculate Landed Cost Manually

If you want to calculate landed cost yourself, use this formula: Landed Cost = Product Cost + Shipping + (Product Cost × Tariff Rate)

Example: A $10 product from China with 30% tariff and $2 shipping = $10 + $2 + ($10 × 0.30) = $15 landed cost. If you sell it for $20, your profit margin is only 25% before other expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tariffs & Import Duties

What is a tariff calculator?
A tariff calculator is a tool that helps e-commerce sellers calculate import duties and taxes on products imported from other countries. It uses your product cost, country of origin, and HTS code to determine the exact tariff rate and landed cost. TariffSnap is a tariff calculator specifically designed for Shopify, Amazon FBA, and Etsy sellers.
How do I calculate landed cost?
To calculate landed cost, add your product cost + shipping cost + (product cost × tariff rate) + any additional fees. For example, a $10 product from China with 30% tariff and $2 shipping = $10 + $2 + ($10 × 0.30) = $15 landed cost. This is the true cost of getting your product to your warehouse.
What are the current tariff rates for China?
As of January 2026, China tariff rates are: General products 30%, Electronics 30%, Textiles 30%, and Postal shipments under $800 are 54%. These rates apply to most e-commerce imports from China to the United States. Use our tariff calculator to see how these rates affect your specific products.
What is an HTS code?
An HTS code (Harmonized Tariff Schedule code) is a 10-digit number that classifies your product for customs purposes. Every product has an HTS code, and the code determines the tariff rate you'll pay. For example, a cotton t-shirt has a different HTS code and tariff rate than a polyester t-shirt. Our tariff calculator helps you find the correct HTS code for your products.
Does TariffSnap work with Shopify, Amazon FBA, and Etsy?
Yes, TariffSnap works with all e-commerce platforms including Shopify, Amazon FBA, Etsy, WooCommerce, and any platform that can export product data to CSV format. Simply upload your SKU list and get instant tariff calculations for your entire catalog.
Why did my tariff rate change in 2026?
Tariff rates changed significantly in 2026 due to new trade policies. China tariffs increased from 25% to 30% for most products, Mexico tariffs went from 0% to 25%, and India textile tariffs jumped to 50%. These changes have a major impact on e-commerce profit margins, which is why you need to recalculate your landed costs now.
What is de minimis?
De minimis is the threshold below which imported goods are exempt from duties and taxes. In the US, the de minimis value used to be $800, meaning shipments under $800 were duty-free. However, as of 2026, postal shipments from China under $800 now have a 54% tariff, effectively eliminating the de minimis benefit for Chinese dropshipping.
How can I reduce my import duties?
To reduce import duties, consider: (1) Sourcing from countries with lower tariff rates like Vietnam (20%) instead of China (30%), (2) Reclassifying products under a different HTS code with a lower rate, (3) Using duty drawback programs if you re-export products, or (4) Discontinuing high-tariff products that are no longer profitable.
