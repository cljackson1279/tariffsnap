Frequently Asked Questions

What is a tariff calculator?

A tariff calculator is a tool that helps e-commerce sellers calculate import duties and taxes on products imported from other countries. It uses your product cost, country of origin, and product category to determine the exact tariff rate and landed cost. TariffSnap is a free tariff calculator specifically designed for Shopify, Amazon FBA, and Etsy sellers.

How do I calculate landed cost?

To calculate landed cost, add your product cost + shipping cost + (product cost × tariff rate) + any additional fees. For example, a $10 product from China with 34.7% tariff and $2 shipping = $10 + $2 + ($10 × 0.347) = $15.47 landed cost. This is the true cost of getting your product to your warehouse.

What are the current tariff rates for China?

As of February 21, 2026, China's effective tariff rate remains 34.7% for most products (up from 2.3% in January 2025). China's tariffs were imposed under Section 232 and fentanyl emergency declarations, which were not affected by the Supreme Court's ruling. Postal shipments under $800 face a 54% tariff rate. Why did my tariff rate change in 2026?

Tariff rates have changed dramatically in 2026. On February 20, 2026, the Supreme Court struck down Trump's IEEPA-based tariffs 6-3. Trump immediately responded by imposing a new 15% global tariff under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974, effective February 24, 2026. This 15% rate now applies to virtually all countries (except USMCA partners Canada and Mexico). China tariffs remain at 34.7% under separate Section 232 authority. India textile tariffs remain at 50%. These changes have a major impact on e-commerce profit margins, which is why you need to recalculate your landed costs now.

How can I reduce my import duties?

To reduce import duties, consider: (1) Leveraging USMCA exemptions for Canada and Mexico imports (0.1% rate vs. 15% global tariff), (2) Checking if your products qualify for exemptions under the new Section 122 tariff (critical minerals, pharmaceuticals, electronics, aerospace), (3) Reclassifying products under a different HTS code with a lower rate, or (4) Discontinuing high-tariff products that are no longer profitable. Note that Vietnam and India now face the same 15% global tariff as most other countries. Our $59 tariff audit shows you exactly which products to keep, kill, or reprice.
